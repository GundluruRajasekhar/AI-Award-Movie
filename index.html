The Price of Love

In a world where respect is earned by salary, one man discovers the true value of human dignity.

Theme Human RelationshipsGenre Sci-Fi Drama / Social DramaRuntime 5–7 minFormat 16:9

Synopsis

In 2028, a person's worth is measured by salary, job title, social status and digital influence. Arjun, a talented but underpaid teacher, watches friends, relatives and even the woman he loves respect wealth more than character.

Driven by rejection, he climbs to become a respected technology leader and learns that money can buy attention, but not genuine relationships. When a crisis strikes, only the people who valued him before his success stand beside him.
