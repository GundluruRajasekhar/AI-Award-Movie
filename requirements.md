# Requirements – The Price of Love

## Project
Short film pitch and production package for the Human Relationships category.
Genre: Sci-Fi / Social Drama. Runtime 5–7 min. Format 16:9 cinematic.

## Functional requirements
1. Show title, tagline, theme, genre, runtime and format.
2. Show the 136-word synopsis.
3. Let viewers move through all 7 scenes (slider and time buttons) and see each scene's visual, dialogue and emotion.
4. Show how Arjun's status and the number of people who truly stand beside him change scene by scene.
5. Show the "why it scores" points and the final screen text.

## Non-functional requirements
- Single self-contained index.html, no external dependencies.
- Responsive from 360px wide upward.
- Light and dark themes via prefers-color-scheme.
- Keyboard accessible with visible focus; scene text announced via aria-live.

## Film production requirements
- 70%+ of shots generated with Seedance; remaining shots, titles and sound edited separately.
- Original inspirational cinematic score for the Scene 5 montage.
- Narration for Scenes 1 and 4; dialogue for Scenes 2, 3, 5, 6, 7.
- Final screen text held for at least 5 seconds.

## Deliverables
- index.html, requirements.md, readme.md, tasks.md
- Deployed link, video deployed link, MVP deployed link (PDF)
